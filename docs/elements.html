Elements元素

Every control this site can render, in the inks, radii and focus ring the pages around it use. None of them is required by a chapter yet; all of them are styled, so a page that one day needs a form does not invent one.本站能渲染的每一种控件，用与正文相同的颜色、圆角与焦点环。它们目前还没有被任何一章用到；样式已经定好，将来哪一页真需要表单，不必另造一套。

01Buttons按钮

Three weights, one shape. The filled button is the single action a page is asking for; the outline is the alternative; the quiet one sits inside prose and does not compete with it.三档轻重，一种形状。实心按钮是页面唯一在请求的动作；描边是另一种选择；安静的那档待在正文里，不与正文抢。

02Text fields文本字段

One pixel of --rule-strong at rest, the ink at 32% on hover, --accent on the focus ring. Placeholder text is the faint ink on purpose: it is an example, not a value.静止时是 1 像素的 --rule-strong，悬停加深到墨色的 32%，焦点环用 --accent。占位文字刻意用最淡的那档墨：它是示例，不是值。

03Selects下拉

The platform arrow is replaced by the same chevron the language switcher draws, so a select opened inside a chapter looks like the one in the top bar.平台自带的箭头被换成语言切换器画的那个 chevron，于是正文里的下拉与顶栏的那一个长得一样。

one of n单选
disabled禁用
list box列表框
Why the top bar does not use one所以顶栏没有用它
The list a <select> opens is painted by the operating system, down to the colour of the highlighted row, and cannot be brought into this palette. The language control is therefore a button and a listbox this stylesheet draws. The selects below are what a form should use where the platform's list is acceptable.<select> 展开的列表由操作系统绘制，连高亮行的颜色都是系统的，无法纳入这套配色。所以顶栏的语言控件是一个按钮加一个自绘列表框。下面的 select 仍然适合那些“系统列表可以接受”的表单。

04Choices选择

A checkbox is a box that fills with the accent and a tick cut out of it; a radio is a ring with a dot; a switch is a checkbox that lies on its side. All three are drawn from the same two tokens, so they cannot drift apart in a dark theme.复选框是对勾从强调色里挖出来，单选框是环加一个点，开关是躺下的复选框。三者都由同样的两个令牌画出来，深色下不会各自走样。

05Ranges and progress滑杆与进度

A range keeps a paper thumb with an accent ring; a progress bar and a meter both read from --rule to --accent, and a meter warns in the second ink when it leaves its optimum.滑杆是纸色滑块加强调色圆环；进度条与度量条都从 --rule 读到 --accent，度量条偏离最优区间时改用第二种墨色。

06Scroll areas滚动区域

Any box that overflows gets the same thumb as the page: thin, transparent until the pointer is over it, and rounded. That is why this rule is written on * rather than on the three containers that happened to scroll first.任何溢出的盒子都用与页面相同的滑块：细、指针移上去才显形、两端是圆的。这也是为什么这条规则写在 * 上，而不是写在最早会滚动的那三个容器上。

07Focus and states焦点与状态

One ring, everywhere一个焦点环，处处相同
Tab through this page: every control answers with a 2px --accent outline at 2px offset. It is set once on the elements, so a control added later cannot forget it.用 Tab 走一遍这一页：每个控件都用 2 像素 --accent、偏移 2 像素的外框作答。规则写在元素上，后来加的控件想忘也忘不掉。
